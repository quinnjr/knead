Core review fixes. `redactValue()` now redacts URLs (their userinfo), `Request`/`Response` objects (method or status, url, headers), objects whose `toJSON()` returns a string, and the own properties of other class instances, with a 20000-node budget per call. `redactCredentials()` uses one normalized key vocabulary for `key=value` pairs and object keys (adding `credential`, `sessionid`, `privatekey`, `api-key`/`x-api-key`, `AccessKey`, `SharedAccessKey`, `pass`, `passphrase`, `jwt` and `sig`). It also redacts JSON- and YAML-shaped `key: value` pairs, quoted values up to their closing quote, `Authorization:`/`Cookie:` header line values whatever their scheme, and URL userinfo after a scheme that starts with a non-letter or runs past 32 characters. The injector stops tracking the transients of a construction that is retried after an async dependency settles. Once `close()` has begun during `initializeLate()`, no further startup hook starts, and an instance whose `onModuleInit` finishes afterwards gets its shutdown hooks. When the cleanup after a failed startup hook also fails, its error is kept as `shutdownError` on the original error. Async factory overrides of `LOGGER`/`DEVELOPMENT` are awaited, and on `resolveOrConstruct()` they raise `ProviderConfigError`. Invalid provider scopes are rejected, and a module's own provider wins over a re-exported child's. `DeferredLogger.attach()` delivers every held warning even when one `warn` throws. `sendRedisCommand()` validates `timeoutMs` and passes `send` a `RedisDeadline`, so `RedisStore` never sends its `EVAL` fallback after the timeout. The new `ownedRedisClientOptions()` is used by both Redis stores; `RedisLockStore`'s own client now has a `connectionTimeout`, and a supplied client is no longer connected by the store.
